Surely you have seen in some movies hackers different devices that they use to hack whatever they want, and you have probably wanted to have one. 
In this post you will learn how to assemble one without having an advanced level in electronics.

### Electronic components we will use:
* 1 ESP32-WROOM (the main component)
* 1 Display SSD1306 (you can also use the SH1106)
* 2 breadboards
* Cables
* 4 small buttons
(It should be noted that this version is the beta, as more blogs are published, we will add more modules, and solder it onto a pcb board to make it portable.)

### Steps to assemble our device:
First we will remove the power buses from our 2 breadboards to join them, so that it looks like this:

![_config.yml]({{ site.baseurl }}/images/protoboard.jpeg)

We will connect our ESP32-WROOM to the board, and we will place our display below the board that we have previously attached:

![_config.yml]({{ site.baseurl }}/images/esp2.jpg)

For the connection we will use the following diagram from teapot174 <https://github.com/Teapot174/ESP-HACK>:

![_config.yml]({{ site.baseurl }}/images/esquema.png)

(For the connections, the pins on the ESP32 are called GPIOs, so don't panic if you don't find this written on your ESP)

Now we will connect the display's VCC port to the 3V3 GPIO pin on the ESP using jumper wires:

![_config.yml]({{ site.baseurl }}/images/esp3.jpg)

Now we will connect the display's GND port to our ESP's GND port:

![_config.yml]({{ site.baseurl }}/images/esp4.jpg)

"Then we will connect the following ports: the display's SCL to GPIO 22 and SDA to GPIO 21 (GPIOs are the connection ports on our ESP32), as shown in the image"
